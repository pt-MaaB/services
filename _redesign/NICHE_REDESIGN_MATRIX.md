## 0. Cross-cutting defects the review found (fix on EVERY niche)

These showed up on all/most pages and matter more than any single headline:

1. **Fabricated star ratings — remove everywhere.** Every hero hardcodes a decorative `★★★★★` row (~line 195) with **no `aggregateRating` in the JSON-LD and no review source**. On a rank-and-rent page that's indefensible (and a legal risk). Delete the star row; replace with operationally-true cues (24h, response time, coverage, certifications).
2. **Broken WhatsApp promise — wire real `wa.me` links.** Every page advertises "WhatsApp ou telefone" in the meta description, Open Graph, schema, FAQ, "Como Funciona" step 1, and final CTA — but **ships zero WhatsApp links; every CTA is `tel:`**. The brief already has the WhatsApp number + prefill. Add real `wa.me` buttons (hero secondary + sticky bar). This fixes a live broken promise, independent of any A/B test.
3. **Reconcile contradictory time-claims.** Several pages state two different times: eletricista hero `<45 min` vs trust bullet `30 min` (line 242); reparação `30 min` bullet vs FAQ `24–48h`. Pick **one coherent time per page** and make every claim match.
4. **Fix the JSON-LD `openingHoursSpecification` 24h lie** on scheduled niches (jardinagem etc.): it claims `00:00–23:59 / 7 days`. Narrow to real hours so structured data matches honest on-page copy — or keep only as *contact* availability, never *intervention*.
5. **Phone numbers: trust the live page, not the brief.** e.g. serralheiro live + schema = `+351 966 077 317` (15 occurrences); the brief's `917 493 600` is stale. Do **not** "correct" the page back to the brief.

## 1. The four archetypes → channel + CTA pattern

| Archetype | Niches | Primary concern | Primary CTA | Channel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Emergency-now** | eletricista, canalizador, serralheiro | speak to a human *now*, risk/damage spreading | **Phone** (concern-framed) + WhatsApp secondary | phone primary; A/B vs WhatsApp |
| **Shame-discreet** | pragas | discreet, fast, no describing it on a call | **WhatsApp** ("falar em privado") | whatsapp primary; phone secondary |
| **Legal-deadline** | limpeza-terrenos | avoid the €5.000 fine before **15 março** | **WhatsApp + form** ("antes do prazo") | whatsapp+form; phone backup |
| **Quote-scheduled** | climatização, jardinagem, mudanças, pintor, reparação-eletrodomésticos | price + reliability, written quote | **"Pedir orçamento grátis"** | whatsapp+form; phone tertiary |

The single biggest copy change across all of them: the primary button stops being a **bare phone number** and starts **naming the concern** (the +68% finding).
